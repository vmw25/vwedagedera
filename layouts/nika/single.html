Question-bank workflow

Wrong answer?
Make it your next card.

Start a PassMedicine session. nika spots the result, picks up the mistake and helps turn it into cards to review. You choose when capture starts. Check your cards before they reach Anki.

How result detection works

A pretrained CNN, a small image-recognition model, helps detect correct and incorrect results. It can make mistakes. The result is a capture aid, not an assessment of your medical knowledge.

Personal, by design

More like you.
Still up to you.

Set your style once. Keep control of every card.

Tell nika how you like to learn. Short answers? Cloze for facts? Hidden labels for anatomy? Your preferences and reviewed edits guide the next batch. Change your defaults any time, or give just one batch a different instruction.

Setup is optional. You can skip it, save your progress and edit your answers later. Sharing examples for model training is a separate, optional choice.

How personalisation works

Pretrained AI creates the cards. Machine-learning tools rank them and find useful examples. This adapts the instructions and examples, not the underlying model. No automatic retraining and no separate model for every user.

A few good
questions.

How will I get started?

Once downloads are available, install nika for your computer. Create your account in the app, confirm your email and sign in. Answer a few short questions, or skip them, then create and review your first cards. Free is your starting plan. Change your plan inside the app when you need more credits.

Do purchases stay inside the app?

You choose your plan or credit top-up in nika. Secure Stripe Checkout or the billing portal may briefly open in your browser to complete payment or manage the subscription. Your plan and credits then update in the app after payment is confirmed.

Do I need Anki?

nika creates cards for Anki; it does not replace Anki’s study scheduler. Direct sync needs Anki desktop and AnkiConnect configured. You can also use the app’s export tools.

Will AI always get the answer right?

No. AI can make mistakes or miss important context. Read each card, check it against your source and edit it before adding it to your deck.

Are my notes used to train a shared model?

Only if you opt in and choose individual, reviewed examples to share. It is never required for a plan. You can withdraw permission and remove your shared examples. Your normal account preferences and private learning examples are separate.

How do AI credits work?

Credits cover AI processing, not a fixed number of cards. Full PDFs and deeper processing can use more credits. Monthly credits reset at the start of each UTC calendar month without rollover, including on annual plans. Welcome and purchased credits carry forward while your account is open. Need more? Add 100 credits for £4.99.

The real nika desktop interface using example study material and example numbers. They show the workflow, not measured customer results.
